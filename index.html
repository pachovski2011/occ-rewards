Transparency

How your money travels

One reason people distrust financial services is that they cannot see what happens after they send money. Here is the actual path we use.

1

You

You decide to send money from the United States. Payment is made by debit card or bank transfer.

2

Ambassador Network

We receive the instruction and coordinate the transfer. We manage the relationship and keep full records.

3

Licensed Guatemalan Payout Partner

Funds are delivered from a local pool through established licensed partners with access to Banrural and secondary banks — the networks families already know and use.

4

Your Family

The recipient collects the money the way they actually need it — bank deposit or cash pickup.

Approach

Why this structure exists

In a market full of similar-looking transfer apps, we chose a different posture: the posture of a small private international financial firm.

Careful operation

We prioritize reliability and judgment over speed claims or the lowest possible fee. Continuity matters more than marketing slogans.

Local on both ends

Value is matched locally. This reduces dependence on long correspondent chains and improves both speed and control.

Personal accountability

The name reflects the founder’s diplomatic background. Trust is treated as an operating principle, not a slogan.

Real families. Real transfers. Clear communication.

Partnership

We work with existing licensed networks

We do not open branches or hire cashiers. We contract with partners who already have access to Banrural, secondary banks, and cash-pickup coverage.

What we look for

Strong Banrural reach (including rural areas), reliable secondary options, transparent commercial terms, and the operational discipline required for high-trust reconciliation. The quality of these relationships largely determines whether real margin exists.

Legal notice & disclaimer

Ambassador Network is not a bank, credit union, or licensed money transmitter. We operate as a coordinator and intermediary. In the initial phase of operations, money movement is executed through established licensed partners who hold the required authorizations.

We do not hold customer funds in our own accounts as a depository institution. All regulated transmission of funds is performed by licensed partners. We provide orchestration, customer relationship management, record-keeping, and reconciliation.

The information on this website is general in nature and does not constitute financial, legal, or regulatory advice. Availability of services, fees, delivery times, and payout methods depend on partner arrangements and applicable regulations.

This website is intended primarily for informational and partnership discussions. Actual transfer services will be offered only in accordance with applicable law and through properly authorized channels.

By using this website you acknowledge that Ambassador Network is not acting as a bank or as a licensed money transmitter in its own right at this stage.
